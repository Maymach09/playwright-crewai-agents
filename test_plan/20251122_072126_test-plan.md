# Application - Feature Test Plan
## Application Overview
This test plan covers the process of creating and deleting an account in Salesforce.

## Test Scenarios
### 1. Create and Delete Account in Salesforce
**Seed:** tests/seed.spec.ts

#### 1.1 Create Account
**Steps:**
1. Navigate to Accounts section.
2. Click on the 'New' button.
3. Fill in the 'Account Name' field with 'Test Account'.
4. Fill in the 'Phone' field with '123-456-7890'.
5. Click the 'Save' button.

**Expected Results:**
- Step 1: Accounts section is displayed.
- Step 2: New account form is displayed.
- Step 3: 'Account Name' is filled with 'Test Account'.
- Step 4: 'Phone' is filled with '123-456-7890'.
- Step 5: Account is created and redirected to the account view page.

#### 1.2 Delete Account
**Steps:**
1. Click on the 'Show more actions' button.
2. Select 'Delete' from the actions menu.
3. Confirm the deletion in the dialog.

**Expected Results:**
- Step 1: Actions menu is displayed.
- Step 2: Account deletion confirmation dialog appears.
- Step 3: Account is deleted and user is redirected to the Accounts list page.
